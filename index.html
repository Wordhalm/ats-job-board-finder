        FAQ
        
    
        
      How can I tell which ATS a company uses?
      
        Look at the address of its job listings. Greenhouse boards live on boards.greenhouse.io or job-boards.greenhouse.io, Lever on jobs.lever.co, Ashby on jobs.ashbyhq.com and SmartRecruiters on jobs.smartrecruiters.com. Many companies hide this behind their own careers page; this index maps 25,787 of them to the underlying board.
        
    
        
    
        
      Where do the open roles come from?
      
        Your browser calls the public job board API of each ATS directly when you open a company. Nothing goes through a server of ours, so the list is as current as the company's own board.
        
    
        
    
        
      Where does the company list come from?
      
        From the open-source kalil0321/ats-scrapers project (MIT License). It is a snapshot: a company that has changed ATS or closed its board will show zero roles.
        
    
        
    
        
      Can I download the whole list?
      
        Yes. The file is data/ats-index.json, each entry is [platform, company name, board slug] with g = Greenhouse, l = Lever, a = Ashby, s = SmartRecruiters.
        
    
        
    
        
      Do you store my searches?
      
        No. The page is static, has no analytics and no cookies. Searches run in your browser.
        
    
        
    
        
      Can I get new job postings from many companies automatically?
      
        Use Career Site Jobs Search on a schedule with "Only new jobs since last run" enabled, or plug it into n8n, Make or Zapier. Ready-made n8n workflows are in clearstack-data-automation-templates.
